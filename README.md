# AquaVision AI

## AI-Based Water Quality, Level and Risk Prediction System

**A Smart Predictive Water Management and Decision Support Platform**

AquaVision AI is a software-based predictive water-management and
decision-support platform designed for dams, reservoirs, lakes, ponds,
and watersheds.

The system integrates water-quality data, historical hydrological data,
and external weather forecasts with Artificial Intelligence and Machine
Learning to predict future water conditions, detect anomalies, assess
risks, explain important prediction factors, and support informed
water-management decisions.

## Core Features

- AI-based water-quality prediction

- Water-quality risk assessment
- Explainable AI using SHAP
- What-if scenario simulation
- Decision-support recommendations
